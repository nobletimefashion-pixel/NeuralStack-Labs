How we work

A clear process, start to finish

No fixed timelines promised before we understand a project. Every engagement moves through the same five stages.

  1. Discovery

    We understand the problem, constraints, available data, and what success actually looks like for you.

  2. Scope

    We agree on deliverables, timeline, assumptions, and acceptance criteria in writing before building.

  3. Build

    Implementation with version control and documented decisions, so nothing is a black box.

  4. Validate

    Testing against agreed requirements and the evaluation metrics that matter for your use case.

  5. Deliver

    The agreed artifacts, documentation, and a proper handover — so your team can take it forward.
