FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What types of projects do you accept?

Projects that fit our four service areas: applied AI/ML, automation and integration, networking and IT engineering, and programming/scripting. We take on work for startups, SMEs, and founders building AI-powered products. If a request falls outside what we can realistically deliver, we will say so rather than take the work.

Can you work with an existing dataset or codebase?

Yes. Most projects start from something that already exists — a dataset with quirks, a codebase with history, or a process that half-works. Part of discovery is understanding what you have before proposing what to change.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. We are remote-first and work with clients across time zones. Communication happens in writing with scheduled calls where they help.

How do you estimate project costs?

After discovery, we provide a written scope with deliverables, assumptions, and a price or price range. Small, well-defined projects can usually be fixed-price. Larger or uncertain ones may be staged, with each stage scoped and priced separately. We do not quote a firm number before understanding the problem — any estimate at that point would be a guess.

Can you build a proof of concept before a larger engagement?

Yes, and we often recommend it. A scoped proof of concept lets both sides test the working relationship and the technical assumptions before a larger commitment. If the PoC shows the idea does not hold, you have spent less finding that out.

How do you protect client data?

We only access the data needed for the agreed scope, handle it carefully, and do not use client data for unrelated purposes. For networking work, all assessments and changes require your explicit authorization. Specific data-handling terms can be written into the project agreement. Owner note: confirm your actual data-handling process and update this answer before launch.

What happens after project delivery?

You receive the agreed artifacts, documentation, and a handover. What happens next — support, iteration, or handover to your own team — is agreed during scoping. Ongoing support can be arranged separately if needed. Owner note: confirm your actual post-delivery policy and update this answer before launch.
