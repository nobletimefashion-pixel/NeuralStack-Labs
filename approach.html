Approach

How we work with you

Five stages, every project. We do not promise fixed timelines before understanding a project — we promise clarity about what happens next.

The five stages

01

Discovery

We start by understanding the problem itself — not the solution you may already have in mind. We look at the constraints, the available data or systems, who will use the result, and what success looks like in measurable terms.

  • Problem framing and constraints
  • Data or system availability review
  • Success criteria definition
02

Scope

Before any build work, we agree on deliverables, timeline, assumptions, and acceptance criteria in writing. If something is out of scope, that is stated explicitly — not discovered halfway through.

  • Written deliverables list
  • Timeline and assumptions
  • Acceptance criteria both sides can check
03

Build

Implementation happens with version control and documented decisions. You can see progress as it happens, and the reasons behind trade-offs are written down rather than living in someone's head.

  • Version-controlled development
  • Documented technical decisions
  • Regular progress updates
04

Validate

We test against the agreed requirements and the evaluation metrics that matter for your use case. For ML work this includes proper validation design and leakage checks; for software, it means tests appropriate to the risk.

  • Testing against acceptance criteria
  • Evaluation metrics relevant to the task
  • Error analysis and known limitations
05

Deliver

You receive the agreed artifacts, documentation, and a proper handover. The goal is that your team can operate and extend the work without being dependent on us.

  • Agreed artifacts and source code
  • Documentation and handover session
  • Known limitations clearly listed

Ready to start at stage one?

Discovery usually starts with a conversation. No commitment required.

Why work with us

Commitments we make on every project

These are not marketing claims — they are working practices. If we cannot uphold one of them on your project, we will tell you before you hire us.

Scope and deliverables agreed in advance

Written scope before build work starts. Changes are handled explicitly, not absorbed silently.

Transparent communication

Regular updates, plain language, and direct answers — including when the answer is "that will not work."

Testing appropriate to the task

Evaluation designed for your decision context — not whichever metric looks most impressive.

Documentation and handover

You get documentation written for the people who will maintain the work after we leave.

Solutions that fit problem and budget

We recommend the simplest approach that works — not the most complex one that inflates an invoice.

Respect for data and authorization boundaries

Client data is handled carefully, and network or system work happens only within explicit, agreed authorization.

Start with a conversation

Tell us about the problem. We will tell you honestly what it would take to solve it.
