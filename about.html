About

A small team that ships

NeauralStack Labs is a seven-member technology services team, including the founder and partners, focused on practical technical delivery for real business problems.

Who we are

We are a small, focused team that works directly with clients. When you hire NeauralStack Labs, you talk to the people doing the work — not a chain of account managers relaying messages.

Our members cover applied AI and machine learning, automation, networking, and software development. For some projects we collaborate with trusted freelancers when a specific skill set is needed; that is always disclosed to the client as part of scoping.

We define realistic project scopes, communicate in language that makes sense to both technical and non-technical stakeholders, and treat engineering discipline — version control, testing, documentation — as non-negotiable.

Some projects are delivered collaboratively with freelancers where appropriate.

How we operate

What working with us looks like

Collaborative by default

We work with you, not around you. Regular updates, shared progress, and questions asked early — because assumptions are more expensive later.

Clear communication

Technical depth without jargon walls. You will always know what stage a project is in, what is blocked, and what decision is needed from you.

Realistic scopes

We would rather scope a small project honestly than overpromise a large one. If your timeline or budget does not support the goal, we say so upfront.

Think we might be a fit?

The best way to find out is a short conversation about your problem.
